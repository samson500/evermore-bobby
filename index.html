About Evermore

Built in Lagos, Nigeria. Built for Africa.

Evermore is a member-based AI training hub headquartered in Lagos, Nigeria. Members contribute to real AI model training — voice training, text annotation, image labeling, and brand feedback review — and get rewarded for their contributions.

No longer location-restricted. You can contribute from anywhere and help build AI that understands African accents, languages, and everyday life.
