Introduction

Evermore Limited (CAC registered RC 8007530) processes personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have.

Information We Collect

We collect your name, email address, transaction records, and platform activity related to your membership and contributions.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to create and manage your account, administer your membership, process activations, and support rewards administration.

Legal Basis

We process personal data on the legal bases of consent and contract, as recognised under the NDPR. Activating membership constitutes agreement to the processing described in this policy.

Data Sharing

We never sell your data. Your information is shared only with our payment partner to process transactions and with service providers necessary to operate the platform.

Data Retention & Security

We retain data only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and encrypted at rest in Firebase.

Your Rights

You may request access to, rectification of, or erasure of your personal data at any time by emailing support@evermore.ng.

Changes & Contact

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates take effect when posted. For questions about this policy, contact support@evermore.ng.
